# 🎮 ULTIMA EDITION

A gaming-focused operating environment that brings your games, emulators, apps, and settings together in one place.

Inspired by the Nintendo Switch, Xbox Dashboard, and Steam Deck, **LNKR SHOCK ULTIMA** is designed to be controller-friendly, fast, and effortless to use. Launch games, manage emulators, personalize your experience, and switch between running games without ever leaving the dashboard.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **Unified Game Library**  
  Access all your games from a single organized dashboard.

- 🕹️ **Emulator Management**  
  Configure and manage your emulators with ease.

- ⚡ **Quick Resume Support**  
  Jump back into your games instantly.

- 🎮 **Controller Auto-Configuration**  
  Plug in your controller and start playing.

- 🖥️ **Instant Fullscreen Launching**  
  Launch directly into your games without distractions.

- 🎨 **Custom Themes & Backgrounds**  
  Personalize LNKR OS to match your style.

- 🚂 **Steam Compatibility Options**  
  Integrate and optimize your Steam gaming experience.

- 🔄 **Running Games Hub**  
  View and switch between active games seamlessly.

- 📥 **Download Manager**  
  Track and manage downloads from one place.

- ✨ **Modern Console-Style Interface**  
  A clean, responsive UI built for gaming.

---
